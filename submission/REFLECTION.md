The anti-pattern our team is most at risk of is treating the lakehouse as a dumping ground. LLM observability and agent traces grow quickly, and without clear Bronze, Silver, and Gold contracts, raw logs, stale embeddings, and orphan files would accumulate silently.

This lab showed that ACID tables are not enough by themselves. We still need operational discipline: schema enforcement at write time, explicit schema evolution, compaction, clustering, vacuum or snapshot expiry, orphan cleanup, and version pinning for reproducible training or replay. The biggest risk is not one failed query; it is slowly losing trust in which data is current, safe, and reproducible.
